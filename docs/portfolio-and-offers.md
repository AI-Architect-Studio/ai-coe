## Merchant and fulfillment evidence

FrankX maintains a Lemon Squeezy checkout implementation. Its activation runbook documents two gated slugs, `starter-kit` and `command-center-template`, plus the order-created webhook and verification sequence. Neither slug identifies the AI CoE offer. Agentic Income references Polar fulfillment in its Blueprint and exposes reservations. This review confirmed a scoped-service inquiry and did not establish a live self-service CoE purchase. No order was placed. [FrankX merchant runbook](https://github.com/frankxai/frankx.ai-vercel-website/blob/0f3968a44fa35d612731e2a46c24c2028c6f1344/docs/commerce/lemon-squeezy-activation.md) · [Agentic Income offers](https://www.agenticincome.ai/offers)

Promote a CoE offer to “buy” only after the owner verifies the SKU, currency, fulfillment artifact revision, applicable license, test payment and customer access. To qualify a managed subscription, verify recurring provisioning and cancellation as well. Use aggregate activation, repeat-run and paid fulfillment counts separately. A download, waitlist signup and payment are different events.

Repository licenses govern their named files. FrankX licensing inquiries cover authored material beyond rights already granted. Keep customer data, brand marks, music, world canon, private curricula and third-party assets under their own terms.
