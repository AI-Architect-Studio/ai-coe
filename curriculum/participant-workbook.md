# Participant workbook

Use one copy per mission in your own workspace. Short answers backed by a file, an observation or a named reviewer are sufficient. Keep your original answers when you revise them.

**Freeze a first attempt.** Write the decision you would make now, one source for it, and one observation that would change your mind. Mark any help you already received. A missing baseline stays unknown.

| Named person’s accept, revise or hold decision | |

| Proposed workflow or skill change, owner and approval state | |

**Transfer.** Ask a peer to alter one assumption without showing the expected decision. Before rerunning, predict which decision or output should change. Record the result, where prediction and observation differed, and what you would now do unaided.

**Return visit.** Name one comparable run within seven days. Freeze a measure such as time to an accepted artifact, corrections per accepted artifact, or success by a second operator. Keep the same task and acceptance condition or record the difference. When no earlier measurement exists, treat this run as the baseline. A peer can challenge a single-run improvement claim.

## Share only your selected evidence

Choose what, if anything, the reviewer may retain. A redacted artifact, an author disclosure and a repeatable command may be enough. Leave private source material, contact lists and unpublished creative work in their owning workspace.
