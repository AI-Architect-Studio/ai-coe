# AI-Native Founder Lab · one day

Bring a product assumption, two permissioned observations and a named person empowered to decide the next experiment. Use the [fictional rehearsal-room founder](../worked-case.md) when real customer data cannot be shown. Complete the workbook before opening the agent. Target one business decision by the afternoon.

| Local session time | Exercise | Artifact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 09:00–10:00 | Write unaided go/revise/stop call; distinguish observed demand from interest | Hypothesis, buyer, baseline and disconfirming result |
| 10:00–11:00 | Activate the founder workspace and inspect a local mission | Working config, source index and first run |
| 11:00–12:15 | Build the cheapest experiment the evidence and authority allow | Decision brief plus a local test artifact |
| 12:15–13:00 | Break | |
| 13:00–14:00 | Peer reviews costs, rights, and claims from two contradictory observations | Annotated critique; measurements distinct from estimates |
| 14:00–15:15 | A source changes or an assumed permission is withdrawn; rerun and revise | New decision and both artifact versions |
| 15:15–15:30 | Break | |
| 15:30–16:30 | Switch operators; defend the experiment to the person who can stop it | Reproducible handoff and explanation of changed judgment |
| 16:30–17:00 | Schedule the narrowest next test | Owner, ceiling, stop rule and 7/30-day dates |

The facilitator plays a skeptical buyer and asks for the source behind every adoption or savings claim. Evaluate whether the founder can abandon an attractive idea on disconfirming evidence. A prototype, a held experiment, a buyer's reply and a payment are separate observations. Keep outreach as a draft until its real owner authorizes sending.
