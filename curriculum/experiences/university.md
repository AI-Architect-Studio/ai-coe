# University AI architecture studio · six weeks

Faculty select one public claim with an inspectable source and a modest reproducible task. Bring a lab machine with the release toolchain and an offline fixture. Invite each student to write an individual pre-assessment and first prediction before team collaboration. Faculty define assistance disclosure, assessment standing and accommodation before enrollment.

| Seminar and lab | Assignment | Faculty check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · 2 hours | Convert the claim into a mission and activate a personal or team workspace | Falsifiable question, original prediction, exact source and scope |
| 2 · 2 hours | Specify context, agent role, available tools and refusal condition | Frozen baseline plan and input set |
| 3 · 2 hours | Run the mission and investigate one failed or ambiguous result | Reproducible artifact and observed cause or open hypothesis |
| 4 · 2 hours | Change one variable and compare the results | Controlled comparison including confounders and review effort |
| 5 · 2 hours | Exchange workspaces for independent reproduction | Reviewer report and author response with revision |
| 6 · 2 hours | Defend a new case individually without agent help, then compare against the replay | Individual judgment, disclosed assistance and second-source review |

Faculty use the [shared review criteria](../facilitator-guide.md) and retain authority over grades, appeals and credit. A team deliverable alone cannot establish individual mastery. Give students a private submission route; publication or external contribution requires a separate rights and consent decision. The proposed studio carries no institutional affiliation.
