# Founder fellowship · six weeks

Use a weekly 90-minute studio and one 60-minute independent block. Each fellow brings a repository they control, one recurring founder decision and a peer willing to operate a workflow. Use the founder lab [workbook](../participant-workbook.md). Agree at intake on ownership, feedback access, permitted assistance and the scope of any portfolio excerpt.

| Week | Studio challenge | Evidence due before the next studio |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Capture unaided business judgment and activate the founder configuration | Mission contract, initial run and time-to-accept baseline or unknown |
| 2 | Run a validation experiment with contrary evidence | Reviewable decision with at least one cited counterexample |
| 3 | Test the same workflow on a materially different case | Versioned skill candidate; failure trace and proposed correction |
| 4 | Pair-run a commercial decision with a spend ceiling | Unit-cost assumptions and a bounded local fulfillment rehearsal |
| 5 | Hand the operation to another fellow without live coaching | Operator log, observed interventions and revised runbook |
| 6 | Defend one changed decision individually; rerun the original benchmark | Before/after packet, limitations and 30-day continuation assignment |

For each studio reserve 15 minutes for unaided predictions, 45 for operating, 20 for reciprocal review and 10 for scheduling the next run. The facilitator may coach on the procedure. Record the degree of assistance. Capture prep, review, retries and visible provider charges when comparing runs.

The exit evaluator observes a peer handoff and a short individual defense on a fresh fact. Report independent operation and founder learning separately. Fellowship participation creates no automatic credential, funding or commercial obligation.
