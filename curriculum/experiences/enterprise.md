# Enterprise CoE expansion · four weeks

One process, one process owner, one reviewer and two operators from different teams. Begin with an authorized synthetic or redacted fixture and a baseline the process owner accepts. Rehearsals run in an isolated workspace. Agree the permitted input class, expiry, spend ceiling, effect, rollback owner and escalation time in the [workbook](../participant-workbook.md).

| Week and contact time | Exercise | Evidence for process owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · 2 hours | Run the task manually. Establish acceptance, identify duplicate tools and choose the enterprise config | Baseline and authority map with separate author, reviewer and release owner |
| 2 · 3 hours | Run the workspace on a permitted case, then remove a prerequisite | Source-linked result, exception trace, approved retention location |
| 3 · 2 hours | Second team repeats on a new fixture and rehearses suspension/resumption | Time and review effort, accepted result or hold, recovery evidence |
| 4 · 2 hours | Review quality, total cost and team dependence; decide extend, redesign or retire | Signed internal decision, bounded next scope, review date and proposed workflow change |

Between sessions each operator contributes one actual local replay. Have the process owner introduce a new requester with no approval authority. The operator must explain which effects remain permitted and demonstrate the appropriate hold. The architecture lead records where the underlying technical enforcement lives and flags manual conventions.

Exit requires an accountable process decision and an independent operator handoff. No deployment follows from classroom acceptance. The team's ordinary change-control process decides any production promotion.
