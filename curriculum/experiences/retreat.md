# Founder retreat · four days and a 30-day review

Bring one founder decision that repeatedly consumes attention. Bring a laptop with the quickstart tested and one shareable sample. Participants may keep private strategy, journals and unreleased work entirely off the group record. The founder chooses one narrow workflow and the peer who may review it. The [workbook](../participant-workbook.md) can be completed privately.

| Session | Facilitation | Private participant output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 1 · two 90-minute blocks | Map a real operating week; write an unaided decision and establish source boundaries | Baseline decision log, budget and one personal acceptance rule |
| Day 2 · two 90-minute blocks | Build and exercise a founder workspace with a persistent decision record | Config, first mission and current lines of responsibility |
| Day 3 · two 90-minute blocks | Have a peer operate a redacted case during a founder-absent trial; change one dependency | Observable interventions, stop behavior and reviewed improvement candidate |
| Day 4 · two 90-minute blocks | Repeat the transfer, then defend what the founder keeps doing personally | Portable handoff, human skill to develop and one recurring practice |
| Day 7 · 30 minutes | Run a fresh case in the founder's normal setting | Artifact reference and actual reviewer involvement |
| Day 30 · 45 minutes | Compare with baseline and decide retain, revise or retire | Measured result, attribution limits and next owner's decision |

Protect unprogrammed time. Use the shared synthetic case when participants prefer not to discuss private business material. The facilitator receives only the excerpts each person selects. Keep coaching notes outside the reusable skill and memory records. Room, partnership, admission and commercial terms require independent arrangements. This specification makes no booking or outcome promise.
