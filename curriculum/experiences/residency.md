# Creative intelligence residency · three days

Each participant brings an original seed work, rights to the source material and a production constraint such as running time, format or instrumentation. Use the creator blueprint with a local source register. The pre-assessment asks each artist to name one creative choice only they can make, and one repeatable task the agent can assist.

| Block | Exercise | Studio artifact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 1 morning | Explain artistic intent; record an unaided decision and applicable source permissions | Private brief, first sketch and rights ledger |
| Day 1 afternoon | Activate the workspace and narrow the production stages | Config, mission and reproducible method for one stage |
| Day 2 morning | Make two versions while varying one creative decision | Versioned exports, provenance and assistance disclosure |
| Day 2 afternoon | Peers critique the work against its stated intention; artist selects a revision | Critique, original version, signed-off creative decision |
| Day 3 morning | Withdraw one source or impose a new format and repeat | New edition, updated rights ledger and failure notes |
| Day 3 afternoon | Hand the production method to a peer and review the result privately | Handoff evidence, learner reflection and next-week practice |

For a music case, replace an uncleared loop with an original performance and verify each output that previously relied on it. For prose, replace a reference passage with a fully original scene and review continuity. The facilitator evaluates reproducibility and rights separately from the artist's judgment of the final work. Export is local. Release, canon admission, licensing and promotion remain separate creator decisions.
