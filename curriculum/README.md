# Teach an AI Center of Excellence

A participant should be able to hand their workspace to someone else, point to the evidence behind its last decision, and explain when they would overrule it. Use the installed CoE on one piece of work the participant owns. Keep the operator’s judgment and the system’s performance as separate observations.

## Run a session

Start with the [participant workbook](participant-workbook.md) and the [facilitator guide](facilitator-guide.md). Rehearse the [repository activation](../README.md) on a new local workspace. Use the [worked case](worked-case.md) when participants cannot bring suitable source material. The case can be completed on paper as a judgment exercise. Record a technical activation as pending whenever it was not run.

| Edition | Audience and working time | What someone else must be able to inspect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Introduction](experiences/introduction.md) | First-time operator, 90 minutes | Workspace, first mission and one decision that changed after review |
| [Activation Lab](experiences/activation-lab.md) | Workflow owners, four hours | Repeatable configuration, source references, failure test and recovery |
| [AI-Native Founder Lab](experiences/founder-lab.md) | Product founders, one day | Decision to pursue, revise or stop a product experiment |
| [Founder fellowship](experiences/fellowship.md) | Small founder cohort, six weeks | Versioned evidence across repeated runs and a peer-operated handoff |
| [Enterprise expansion](experiences/enterprise.md) | Process owner, architecture lead and operator, four weeks | Scoped authority, exception behavior, cost record and team handoff |
| [University architecture studio](experiences/university.md) | Faculty-led teams, six seminars and labs | Reproducible project and individual defense on a changed input |
| [Creative intelligence residency](experiences/residency.md) | Artists, writers and producers, three days | Original private edition, rights ledger and reproducible production method |
| [Founder retreat](experiences/retreat.md) | Founder and peer operator, four days plus a 30-day review | Operating notebook, delegated trial and evidence of continued use |

These are open teaching designs. Delivery dates, accreditation, paid enrollment, and participant results require separate evidence.

## Existing teaching infrastructure

Use the Academy's learning quest and record checker for optional structured practice. Keep its existing schema and plugin in their upstream repository. The [Academy bridge and replay](academy-bridge.md) gives the exact revision, commands and verification boundary. For deeper engineering practice, the existing FrankX operating systems workshop offers a twelve-lesson sequence with an offline lab. The curriculum source identifies the workshop route at [Build an AI operating system](https://frankx.ai/workshops/ai-operating-systems).

An educator can deliver the materials in a local editor. An institution chooses its own supervision, evaluation and record-retention rules. Participants keep control of their work. Publishing a case study is a separate decision after the exercise.
