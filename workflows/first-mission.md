# First mission · from observations to a decision

The owner declares an audience, decision, candidate actions, proposed experiments and evidence on both sides. The runner checks the exact excerpts against local UTF-8 files and records each source digest. Candidates are ordered by distinct supporting file digests. An alphabetical tie-break stabilizes display only. A tie or an absence of support sends the decision back for evidence.

Read the strongest challenge before accepting an experiment. A source file may summarize several interviews, and several files may describe one interview. The count measures supplied artifacts. The owner judges independence, contradictions, feasibility and rights. Record the decision, rationale, reviewer and review date in the generated decision brief. A passing mechanical check leaves the decision pending.

To repeat, supply fresh evidence and activate a new empty workspace. Compare the two receipts and record what changed, including human interventions and time to an accepted result. Propose any transferable improvement through the existing intake, design, build, review, deploy and monitor process. The runner writes proposals only. Shared policies, skills and external actions require their owners' separate review.

Activation includes an exact local snapshot of each supplied evidence file and a relocatable intake in `replay/`. Select and redact the complete files before applying. To reproduce the mission, pass `.ai-coe/replay/input.json` as the intake for a new workspace. Equal or greater counts of challenging versus supporting source digests on the leader require the owner to resolve conflicting evidence. Stronger contrary evidence can outweigh multiple weak sources at review regardless of counts.
