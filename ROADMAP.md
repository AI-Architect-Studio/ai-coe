# Public roadmap

Proposed milestones measured from acceptance of this release. Owners below are operating roles; dates do not constitute customer commitments.

| Window | Accountable role | Deliverable | Acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 7 days | Maintainer and independent facilitator | Reproduce from a clean checkout on a second OS. Deliver the 90-minute pilot. Reconcile access links on the three existing sites. | Two machine receipts, one consented learner artifact and one recorded revision. Merge only after review. |
| 30 days | Academy curriculum lead and operator | Run founder and creator labs with distinct owners. Test explicit export/import to one upstream memory capability, record costs for one authorized model-assisted mission. | Second operator repeats the mission. Before/after comparison, redacted transfer receipt and approved source rights. |
| 90 days | Architecture, commercial and institutional owners | Evaluate enterprise delegation and a university cohort. Pilot a scoped professional implementation. | Signed boundaries, an independently reviewed handoff, one accepted customer outcome, reproducible unit cost and verified fulfillment before introducing a purchase CTA. |

Community contributions can reproduce, challenge or improve any of these measurements. Keep proposals in the existing repository review process.
