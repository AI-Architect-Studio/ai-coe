# The rebellion is creation.

A studio owner should be able to answer a colleague who asks, six months later, “Why did we build this?” A researcher should be able to leave a provider and take the work with her. A team should know which decision its agent may make, what that decision costs and who can reverse it.

Those are design requirements. Keep the sources. Write down the judgment. Put a real artifact in someone else's hands. Teach them enough that they can run the next mission without you.

Build and evolve your AI Center of Excellence. For a founder, build the intelligence infrastructure behind your next company. For a creator, create what doesn't exist yet. Let the work carry the claim.
