# Creator AI CoE

Choose an original production brief and credit its sources. The owner supplies a goal, candidate actions and attributed observations. The first mission builds a creative brief and exposes both supporting and challenging evidence.

Start with the existing [creator-business operating template](../../templates/creator-business/README.md) and the [builder-agent role](../../agents/builder-agent.md). Use the [shared activation commands](../../README.md#activate-a-workspace) with `"audience": "creator"` in your intake. Review the [blueprint configuration](blueprint.json) for priority domains, suggested skills and decision rights.

At review, answer “Which original element will I prototype and who owns the source material?” Sign off locally before assigning a follow-up. Repeat with fresh observations to compare the decision.
